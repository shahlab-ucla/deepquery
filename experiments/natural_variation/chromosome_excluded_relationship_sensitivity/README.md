# Chromosome-excluded relationship sensitivity

Question: does LD-pruned relatedness estimated outside the tested chromosome improve
calibration?

Six chromosome-complement relationship matrices feed 48 chromosome-level fits spanning four
traits and two fixed-effect endpoints. Chunks are assembled into eight genome-wide maps.

Status: complete. The relationship sensitivity generally does not resolve the observed
inflation.

Implementation: `wormctx.poc.qtl_grm_sensitivity`.
